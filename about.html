About Me

Biography

I am a Quantum Computing Libraries Engineer at NVIDIA and hold a PhD in Quantum Computing from the Technical University of Munich. My work spans quantum full-stack toolchain development, HPC acceleration, and open-source software.

At NVIDIA, I focus on quantum chemistry software, quantum error correction, and real-time systems across heterogeneous architectures (CPU, GPU, QPU).

During my PhD, I developed MQT Qudits — the first open-source framework for mixed-dimensional quantum computing — along with novel compilation and simulation methods for high-dimensional quantum systems.

I have experience in C++, Python, HPC programming with MPI, OpenMP, CUDA, FPGA design, and multi-GPU cluster optimization. I enjoy translating theoretical advances into robust, practical products.
